Brainstroke Prediction

Early prediction helps prevent serious health risks.

🚶

Stay Active

Regular physical activity like walking, jogging, or yoga helps improve blood circulation, strengthens the heart, and significantly reduces the risk of stroke and other cardiovascular diseases.

🥗

Healthy Diet

Maintain a balanced diet rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and low-fat foods. Avoid excess salt, sugar, and processed foods to keep your body and brain healthy.

🚭

No Smoking

Smoking damages blood vessels, increases blood pressure, and raises the risk of clot formation. Quitting smoking can greatly reduce your chances of stroke and improve overall health.

💓

Control BP

High blood pressure is one of the leading causes of stroke. Monitor your BP regularly and follow medical advice to keep it under control.

🧠

Checkup

Regular health checkups help detect early warning signs and risk factors. Early diagnosis and treatment can prevent serious complications like stroke.
